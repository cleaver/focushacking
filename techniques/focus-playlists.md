## Focus Playlists

Pre-curated playlists of instrumental music — specifically classical, lo-fi, ambient, or video game soundtracks — played during focused work to reduce the signal-to-noise ratio of the workspace without the attentional burden of lyrics. Consistent playlists also serve as behavioral cues.

### Example

Spotify and YouTube offer dedicated 'Deep Focus' and 'Lo-Fi Beats' playlists with hundreds of millions of streams. Game composers like Austin Wintory and Jeremy Soule produce soundtracks specifically designed to support sustained mental engagement.
