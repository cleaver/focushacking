## Coherent Breathing (Resonance Breathing)

Breathing at a rate of approximately 5–6 breaths per minute (5 seconds in, 5 seconds out) to maximize heart rate variability (HRV) — a measure of autonomic nervous system balance strongly linked to cognitive performance and self-regulation capacity.

### Example

Elite athletes and military personnel use coherent breathing as part of HRV biofeedback training. HeartMath Institute uses it as the core intervention in their cognitive performance programs adopted by corporations including Cisco and Stanford Health.

## Papers

### 2023

1. **Effect of coherent breathing on mental health and wellbeing: a randomised placebo-controlled trial** — *RCT*
   - Authors: Rosen B et al.
   - Effect: No effect
   - In the largest blinded breathwork RCT to date, coherent breathing at ~5.5 breaths/min showed no measurable effect on stress, mental health, sleep, or wellbeing over 4 weeks compared to a matched active placebo breathing condition at 12 breaths/min.
   - [Link](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-023-49279-8)

### 2022

1. **Effect of Resonance Breathing on Heart Rate Variability and Cognitive Functions** — *RCT*
   - Authors: Bharati A et al.
   - N=50
   - Effect: Moderate increase
   - Four weeks of daily 20-minute resonance frequency breathing significantly improved HRV (SDNN, pNN50, total power), Trail Making Test A and B performance, and reduced perceived stress compared to a non-intervention control group.
   - [Link](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8924557/)

### 2018

1. **How Breath-Control Can Change Your Life: A Systematic Review on Psycho-Physiological Correlates of Slow Breathing** — *Systematic review*
   - Authors: Zaccaro A et al.
   - Effect: Moderate increase
   - Breathing at resonance frequency (~0.1 Hz, ~6 breaths/min) consistently and specifically maximized HRV amplitude and baroreflex sensitivity compared to other breathing rates, with downstream psychological flexibility benefits.
   - [Link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30245619/)
