## Progressive Muscle Relaxation (PMR)

Edmund Jacobson's technique of systematically tensing and releasing each major muscle group in sequence. By producing a deep physical relaxation state, PMR reduces the bodily tension and cortisol that impair prefrontal cortex function and sustained attention.

### Example

Olympic athletes, test pilots, and surgeons use PMR before high-stakes performances. Some elite chess players (including world champions) use abbreviated versions before tournament games to clear physical tension that interferes with analytical thinking.
