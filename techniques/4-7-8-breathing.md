## 4-7-8 Breathing

Developed by Dr. Andrew Weil, this technique involves inhaling for 4 counts, holding for 7 counts, and exhaling for 8 counts. The extended exhale activates the parasympathetic nervous system, rapidly reducing anxiety and creating a calm, focused state.

### Example

Dr. Andrew Weil recommends 4-7-8 breathing before high-pressure presentations or exam situations. Anesthesiologists and emergency physicians use modified versions to quickly reset after stressful cases before returning to cognitive tasks.

## Papers

### 2023

1. **The Effect of Deep Breathing Exercise and 4-7-8 Breathing Techniques Applied to Patients After Bariatric Surgery on Anxiety and Quality of Life** — *RCT*
   - Authors: Kurt Aktaş G & Eskici İlgin V
   - N=63
   - Effect: Moderate increase
   - The 4-7-8 breathing group showed significantly lower post-test state anxiety scores than both the deep breathing group and control group after bariatric surgery.
   - [Link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36480101/)

### 2018

1. **How Breath-Control Can Change Your Life: A Systematic Review on Psycho-Physiological Correlates of Slow Breathing** — *Systematic review*
   - Authors: Zaccaro A et al.
   - Effect: Moderate increase
   - Slow breathing techniques (<10 breaths/min) consistently increased HRV and EEG alpha power while reducing anxiety and improving psychological well-being across multiple studies in healthy subjects.
   - [Link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30245619/)

### 2002

1. **Slow breathing increases arterial baroreflex sensitivity in patients with chronic heart failure** — *Randomized crossover*
   - Authors: Bernardi L et al.
   - N=102
   - Effect: Moderate increase
   - Slow breathing at 6 breaths/min significantly increased baroreflex sensitivity and reduced blood pressure, demonstrating the autonomic mechanism underlying slow, extended-exhale breathing protocols.
   - [Link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11790690/)
