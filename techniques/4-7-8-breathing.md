## 4-7-8 Breathing

Developed by Dr. Andrew Weil, this technique involves inhaling for 4 counts, holding for 7 counts, and exhaling for 8 counts. The extended exhale activates the parasympathetic nervous system, rapidly reducing anxiety and creating a calm, focused state.

### Example

Dr. Andrew Weil recommends 4-7-8 breathing before high-pressure presentations or exam situations. Anesthesiologists and emergency physicians use modified versions to quickly reset after stressful cases before returning to cognitive tasks.
