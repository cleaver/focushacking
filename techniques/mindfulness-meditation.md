## Mindfulness Meditation

A breath-anchored attention practice where you observe thoughts, sensations, and sounds without judgment, repeatedly redirecting wandering attention back to the breath. Regular practice strengthens the neural circuitry of sustained focus.

### Example

A surgeon meditates for 15 minutes before complex procedures, reporting enhanced intraoperative focus and reduced anxiety. Emergency medicine physicians use brief sessions between cases to reset attention.
