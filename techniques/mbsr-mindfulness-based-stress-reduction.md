## MBSR (Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction)

Jon Kabat-Zinn's structured 8-week program combining formal meditation, body scan, mindful yoga, and group discussion to fundamentally rewire stress response and attentional control. The gold standard evidence-based mindfulness intervention.

### Example

Google, General Mills, and Aetna have implemented MBSR-based programs for employees. Aetna estimated the program saved approximately $2,000 per employee in healthcare costs and added $3,000 in productivity annually.
