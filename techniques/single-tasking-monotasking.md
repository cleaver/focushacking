## Single-Tasking (Monotasking)

Deliberately performing one task at a time with complete attention, refusing to switch tasks until the current one is either complete or intentionally paused. Counteracts the multitasking myth with a structured cognitive discipline that preserves working memory capacity.

### Example

Author Ryan Holiday writes one book at a time, with a single document open, no internet, for 3 hours each morning — a discipline he credits with producing consistently high-quality work on a prolific schedule.
