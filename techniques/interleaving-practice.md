## Interleaving Practice

Deliberately mixing different (but related) topics or skills within a single study or practice session, rather than completing one topic before moving to the next. Interleaving forces the brain to continuously discriminate between concepts, dramatically improving retention and transfer.

### Example

Medical students at Johns Hopkins use interleaved spaced repetition cards mixing pharmacology, anatomy, and pathology — a direct application shown to improve USMLE board exam scores. Language learners using Duolingo experience interleaving by design.
