## Walking in Nature

Taking a 20–40 minute walk in a natural environment (park, forest, riverside) to invoke involuntary fascination that allows directed attention to replenish. Kaplan's Attention Restoration Theory (ART) explains this as softly fascinating stimuli allowing the voluntary attention system to recover.

### Example

Charles Darwin walked the 'Thinking Path' (a sandwalk) around his garden daily to restore attentional clarity between long writing sessions. Steve Jobs was well known for walking meetings in nature to promote creative thinking.
