## Pomodoro Technique

Work in focused 25-minute intervals (pomodoros) separated by 5-minute breaks, with a longer 20–30 minute break after every four cycles. This rhythm leverages the brain's natural attention cycles to sustain concentration while preventing mental fatigue.

### Example

A software developer uses Pomodoro to tackle coding sprints; each 25-minute block is dedicated to one feature, preventing context-switching and ensuring frequent mental recovery breaks.
