## Desk Stretching Routine

A structured sequence of 5–10 minutes of targeted stretches performed every 60–90 minutes of seated work, reducing the physical tension and postural stress that erode cognitive focus by activating the parasympathetic nervous system and increasing blood flow.

### Example

Software teams at Amazon and Spotify incorporate 'movement breaks' every 60 minutes into their engineering sprint culture, reporting fewer afternoon productivity crashes and lower rates of repetitive strain injury.
