## Time Blocking

Assign every hour of the workday to a specific task or category on a calendar, so every minute has a designated purpose. This eliminates reactive decision-making about what to do next and protects deep-work periods from interruption.

### Example

Cal Newport, Georgetown computer science professor, uses daily time-block plans where every hour is assigned, allowing him to write multiple books while maintaining a full academic workload.
