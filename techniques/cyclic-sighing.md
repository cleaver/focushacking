## Cyclic Sighing

A Stanford-validated technique consisting of two inhales through the nose (a full inhale, followed by a short secondary inhale to fully inflate the lungs) followed by a long, complete exhale through the mouth. The extended exhale maximally activates the parasympathetic nervous system.

### Example

Stanford neurobiologist Andrew Huberman teaches cyclic sighing as the fastest real-time tool for restoring calm focus. First responders and combat controllers use the technique between high-intensity events to rapidly reset cognitive clarity.
