## Cognitive Offloading (GTD System)

David Allen's Getting Things Done (GTD) framework treats the human mind as a processing unit, not a storage unit. By systematically capturing all open loops (commitments, ideas, tasks) into a trusted external system, working memory is freed for focused attention rather than constant task tracking.

### Example

David Allen himself, engineers at IBM, and government officials in Europe have implemented GTD. Software developer Kent Beck describes GTD as responsible for recovering 2+ hours of daily focus by eliminating the mental overhead of tracking open commitments.
