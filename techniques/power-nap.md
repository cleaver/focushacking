## Power Nap

A brief 10–20 minute sleep in the early afternoon that rapidly restores alertness, working memory, and attention without the 'sleep inertia' grogginess caused by longer naps. Timed to the post-lunch circadian dip, it functions as an attentional reset.

### Example

NASA research on military pilots and astronauts showed 40-minute naps improved performance by 34% and alertness by 100%. Companies including Google (nap pods), Nike, and Ben & Jerry's provide designated napping spaces for employees.
