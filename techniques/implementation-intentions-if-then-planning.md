## Implementation Intentions (If-Then Planning)

A goal-pursuit strategy where you pre-specify the exact situational cue ('if') that will trigger a specific goal-directed behavior ('then'), linking the plan to environmental triggers. This dramatically increases the probability of initiating focused work when the planned situation arises.

### Example

Personal trainer and writer James Clear uses implementation intentions in his habit-formation framework: 'I will [behavior] at [time] in [location]' — a formula used by millions via his book Atomic Habits.
