## Visualization (Mental Rehearsal)

The systematic mental rehearsal of the successful completion of a task or performance, activating neural pathways as if the experience were real. Used by athletes, surgeons, and executives to prime focused execution by clarifying what specific attention should be directed toward.

### Example

Surgeon Tony Yuen visualizes the full surgical procedure the morning before complex operations, mentally rehearsing critical decision points. Olympic swimmer Michael Phelps visualized races in precise detail before competing.
