## Diaphragmatic Breathing

Breathing that uses the diaphragm — allowing the belly to expand on inhale rather than just the chest — at a slow, deliberate pace. This activates the parasympathetic nervous system, reduces cortisol, and directly improves sustained attention.

### Example

Voice actors, broadcasters, and singers use diaphragmatic breathing as their primary professional focus tool. Yoga instructors teach it as the foundation of pranayama practice for cognitive enhancement.
