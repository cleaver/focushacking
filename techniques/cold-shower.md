## Cold Shower

Ending a shower with 1–3 minutes of cold water exposure to trigger a noradrenaline release that increases alertness and mental energy. Cold exposure activates the sympathetic nervous system, producing a physiological state similar to caffeinated alertness.

### Example

Entrepreneurs including Tony Robbins and Tim Ferriss use daily cold showers as a 'activation ritual' before high-stakes work. Distance swimmer Lewis Pugh uses Wim Hof–style cold exposure to prepare for mentally demanding open-water swims.
