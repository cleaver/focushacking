## Mindful Journaling

Writing freely about present thoughts, feelings, and experiences with deliberate, non-judgmental awareness. Unlike gratitude journaling, mindful journaling uses writing as a vehicle for present-moment exploration rather than reflection, clearing mental clutter before focused work.

### Example

Morning pages — three pages of longhand stream-of-consciousness writing popularized by Julia Cameron — are used by writers like Tim Ferriss and creatives worldwide as a pre-work mental clearing ritual.
