## Notification Batching

Configuring or manually restricting smartphone notifications to be delivered only at 2–3 scheduled times per day (e.g., 9 AM, 1 PM, 6 PM) rather than in real-time. This converts unpredictable interruptions into predictable, contained events that protect cognitive flow.

### Example

Writer and investor Kevin Kelly checks email three times per day on a schedule he publicly shares with correspondents. HBR research on 'email batching' found executives who checked email 3x/day reported 26% lower stress than those checking continuously.
